Science & data limitations

Only NISAR-derived radar data may support measured claims. GCOV backscatter difference (dB) cannot directly prove a wildfire, smoke concentration, subsidence in cm, or human impacts.

Current app mode: SYNTHETIC. All synthetic test layers and observations are fictional and should never be presented as NASA measurements.

When live mode is connected, validation must confirm compatible orbit, grid, polarization and quality masks before analysis.
