**00:00–00:15 — Visualize**: Open the workspace. Show reference/comparison observation labels and pair QA. Drag the Radar Reveal slider to contrast before-like grayscale with difference colors. State: "Red/blue are positive/negative radar backscatter differences in dB, not fire risk."

**00:15–00:30 — Investigate**: Drag on the radar canvas to select a bounded AOI. Press **Analyze selected region**. Tool trace shows list scenes → validate pair → compute → summarize → fingerprint → provenance.

**00:30–00:50 — Understand**: In AI Earth Investigator, read one Observed claim with actual tool-produced number. Click its `SYN-...` chip. Provenance modal displays source granule placeholder, UTC timestamps, pair, AOI, calibration method and limitations.

**00:50–01:10 — Communicate**: Press **Build evidence story**. Walk through 5 frames: Before, Change, Investigation, Known & Unknown, Tell the Story. Click a frame to focus the radar view; click Evidence to return to source.

**01:10–01:20 — Scientific trust**: Change AOI; show existing story switches to `STALE` until evidence is recalculated. Explain that the system refuses to infer cause or fire damage from GCOV intensity alone.

**01:20–01:30 — Close**: "NISAR measures surface change. SAR2Action makes every insight visible, understandable, and traceable: from pixels to evidence, from evidence to stories."
